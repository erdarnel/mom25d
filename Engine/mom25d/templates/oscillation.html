  • Loop gain = level difference (aggressor downstream - victim upstream, with at least one gain stage between them) + coupling; every loop at its worst phase.
  • Line-line: bare-microstrip quasi-static coupling (no side grounds, so an upper bound for GCPW / via-fenced lines unless a layer's coupling offset was fitted), runs summed coherently; layers are assumed shielded from each other by solid planes.
  • Via radiation: vertical current element with its ground image linking upstream lines (x2 for the electric part), times the ground-via fence transmission. Via cavity: radial parallel-plate wave between signal vias, times both fences.
  • Not modelled: coupling between different chains, bias / control lines, part-internal feedback.
